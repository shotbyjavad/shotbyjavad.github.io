Holy Bethlehem Church

The Holy Bethlehem Church is a historic Armenian church located in Isfahan, Iran. It is known for its beautiful architecture and intricate ceiling designs.

The church serves as a place of worship for the Armenian community in the region and is a testament to the rich cultural heritage of Isfahan.
